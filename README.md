# Global Employee Share Plan – Data Validation & Reconciliation Analysis

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Excel analytics project simulating operational support for a global employee share-plan programme.

The project focuses on the core responsibilities commonly found in share-plan operations: data accuracy, record maintenance, transaction validation, payroll reconciliation, exception investigation, regulatory/approval controls and business reporting.

> **Important:** This is a synthetic portfolio project. It does not contain LSEG confidential data or represent LSEG's actual internal processes.

## Business Scenario
A global organisation operates employee share plans across multiple countries. The operations team needs to review transaction files, validate employee and transaction data, reconcile share-plan tax deductions with payroll, identify exceptions and provide management with an actionable operational view.

## Key Questions
- Are employee and transaction records complete and valid?
- Which transactions require investigation before processing?
- Where are payroll and share-plan tax values inconsistent?
- Which countries and share plans generate the most operational activity?
- What issues should be prioritised for follow-up?

## Dataset
- 2,000 employee records
- 6,000 share-plan transactions
- 2,335 payroll records
- 7,548,491 positive share units
- £376,813,391 simulated gross transaction value
- 583 validation exceptions (9.7%)
- 2,486 payroll records requiring reconciliation review

## Workbook Structure
1. **Dashboard** – management KPIs, exception analysis and transaction activity.
2. **Employee_Data** – employee master dataset.
3. **Share_Transactions** – transaction-level share-plan data.
4. **Payroll_Data** – simulated payroll deductions.
5. **Validation_Checks** – automated data-quality controls.
6. **Reconciliation** – share-plan vs payroll tax reconciliation.
7. **Business_Insights** – operational interpretation and investigation actions.
8. **Data_Dictionary** – field definitions and business meaning.

## Business Outcome
The workbook converts raw operational records into an exception-driven workflow: identify the issue, quantify its impact, determine the likely business action and support follow-up with Payroll, Finance, Tax, People and other partner teams.
